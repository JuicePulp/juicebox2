# agents.txt — automated-agent guide for Juicebox (https://box.juicey.dev)

What Juicebox is:
  Juicebox is a free file host. You upload a file and get a short shareable
  link with a few flavors: preview page, raw file bytes, and forced download.
  Uploads expire after a configurable retention period (e.g. 30 minutes to
  7 days).

How to use it:
  1. Read the machine-readable API contract first:
     https://box.juicey.dev/api/openapi.json
  2. Uploads are link-public: anyone with the link can fetch the file until
     it expires. Do not upload or redistribute illegal, abusive, or malicious
     content.
  3. Be polite: cache responses, respect rate limits and retry-after headers,
     identify your bot with a descriptive User-Agent including contact info.

What is off-limits:
  - Per /robots.txt content signals: search indexing is fine (yes), but
    feeding site content into AI models (ai-input) or training/fine-tuning
    AI models on it (ai-train) is NOT permitted. These restrictions are an
    express reservation of rights under Article 4 of EU Directive 2019/790.
  - /admin/ and non-public API routes are not for automated use.
